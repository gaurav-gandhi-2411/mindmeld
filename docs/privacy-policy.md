# Mindmeld Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 2026-06-12

This policy describes how Mindmeld ("the app", "we") handles your data. Mindmeld is a free word-guessing game. We do not require accounts or collect personal information beyond what is described below.

---

## SDKs Integrated and Data Collected

### Google AdMob + UMP (User Messaging Platform)

**Purpose:** Serve rewarded ads that unlock in-game hints.

**Data collected:**
- Advertising identifier (GAID on Android) — used by Google to personalise or limit ads.
- Device and app metadata (OS version, app version, language) — standard ad-serving signals.
- Ad interaction events (ad shown, reward earned, ad skipped).

**Consent:** In regions covered by GDPR (EEA/UK), a UMP consent dialog is shown before the first ad loads. Ads are served in a non-personalised mode if consent is denied or not required.

**Google's privacy policy:** https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

### RevenueCat

**Purpose:** Process and validate in-app purchases (Remove Ads, Archive Access, Streak Freeze packs).

**Data collected:**
- Anonymous app user ID generated by RevenueCat on first launch — not linked to any real-world identity.
- Purchase tokens and transaction IDs provided by the Google Play Store — used to verify and restore purchases.
- Entitlement status (which products are active).

**What we do NOT collect through RevenueCat:**
- Payment card details — these stay entirely within the Google Play Store.
- Your name, email, or any contact information.

**RevenueCat's privacy policy:** https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

---

### Firebase Analytics

**Purpose:** Understand aggregate gameplay patterns (e.g. guess counts, hint usage, win rates per language) to guide future content decisions.

**Data collected:**
- Anonymous session and device identifiers generated by Firebase — not linked to any real-world identity.
- In-game analytics events:
  - `guess_made` — a guess was submitted (no word text is sent; only the temperature band and guess count).
  - `hint_used` — a hint was revealed (hint index and rank; no word text).
  - `win` — the puzzle was solved.
  - `ad_watched` — a rewarded ad completed (whether it was a fallback ad or live).
  - `share_tapped` — the share button was tapped.
  - `purchase` — an in-app purchase was completed (product name only).
- Language selection (`en` or `hi-en`) and puzzle date (`yyyy-MM-dd`), attached to every event.

**What we do NOT send via Firebase Analytics:**
- The secret word for the day.
- Any text you type into the guess field.
- Device location beyond coarse country/region (standard Firebase behaviour).

**Offline queue:** Events are stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database first. When connectivity is available and analytics is enabled, they are flushed to Firebase in batches. Events are retained locally for up to 500 entries (FIFO ring buffer).

**Firebase's privacy policy:** https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

---

### Firebase Crashlytics

**Purpose:** Detect and diagnose app crashes so we can fix them quickly.

**Data collected:**
- Crash traces and stack frames (code-level, not user content).
- Device model, OS version, and app version at the time of the crash.
- A randomly generated installation ID to deduplicate crash reports.

**What we do NOT collect through Crashlytics:**
- Any gameplay state (guesses, hints, scores).
- Any text you typed.
- Any personally identifiable information.

**Firebase's privacy policy:** https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

---

## What We Do NOT Collect

- **No accounts.** Mindmeld has no login, registration, or account system.
- **No contacts or address book access.**
- **No location data** beyond the coarse country/region Firebase infers from IP address (standard Firebase behaviour; we do not request location permission).
- **Gameplay stays on device** — guess history, streaks, and stats are stored only in the local SQLite database on your device. They are not uploaded to any server (except as aggregate, anonymised analytics events described above).

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

- **Local data** (guess history, streaks, analytics ring buffer): stored on your device. You can clear it at any time by uninstalling the app.
- **Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data** is retained for up to 14 months per Google's default settings.
- **RevenueCat purchase records** are retained for the period required to support purchase restoration and disputes.

## GDPR and UMP Consent

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom, we rely on Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect consent before showing personalised ads. You may change or withdraw your consent at any time via the app's **Settings → "Privacy options"** entry, which reopens the Google UMP privacy options form (implemented via the standard `google_mobile_ads` UMP `showPrivacyOptionsForm` mechanism). Ads will be served in a non-personalised mode if consent is denied or withdrawn.
