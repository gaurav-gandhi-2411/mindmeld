## Full Description

<!-- Character count: 2720 / 4000 -->

```
How close is your guess to the secret word?

Warmer is a daily semantic word puzzle game — a new kind of semantic word
guessing challenge where you guess any word and the game tells you how
semantically close it is to the hidden answer. Every guess gets a rank
(1 = secret word) and a temperature: Scorching, Hot, Warm, Cold, or Freezing.
No letter clues. No grid. Pure word association game logic.

A new puzzle drops every day in English and Hinglish. Come back tomorrow.
Keep your streak alive.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. Type any word you know.
2. See your rank — how close that word is in meaning to the secret.
3. Use temperature feedback to steer: Scorching means you are almost there.
4. Solve in as few guesses as you can, or use a hint when you are stuck.
5. Share your result — a spoiler-free emoji card shows your path without
   revealing the word.

HINGLISH MODE — INDIA KA APNA PUZZLE
--------------------------------------
Warmer includes a full Hinglish word game edition built from the ground up
for Indian players. The secret word and the entire vocabulary are in
romanized Hinglish — words like dost, khushi, safar. The semantic model
understands that dost is closer to friend than to paisa, so the
rank+temperature mechanic works the way you expect it to. Switch between
English and Hinglish in Settings any time. Both editions are included — no
extra download.

Note: a hi-IN localized store listing is planned for Phase 3. The current
listing is in English; Hinglish mode is available inside the app from day one.

STREAKS AND STATS
-----------------
Your daily streak, best streak, average guess count, and win rate are tracked
locally on your device — no account needed, no sign-in. A streak freeze lets
you protect a streak on a day you cannot play.

HINTS
-----
Stuck? Watch a short rewarded ad to reveal a hint word closer to the answer.
Up to five hints per puzzle. Players who remove ads get hints without watching
anything.

OFFLINE PLAY
------------
Seven days of puzzles are bundled inside the app. No internet connection
needed to play — the app fetches new puzzles in the background when you are
online and falls back to the bundle when you are not. All of this runs with no
server-side inference: the semantic model is precomputed offline, so the game
works at any connection speed.

FREE WITH OPTIONAL UPGRADES
----------------------------
Warmer is free to play. Optional one-time purchases:
- Remove Ads: hints without rewarded ads, forever.
- Archive Access: play any past puzzle you missed.
- Streak Freeze Pack: protect your streak for a missed day.

This is a brain training puzzle built for people who love words, meaning, and
the daily ritual of a single clean challenge.
```
