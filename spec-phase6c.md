# Mindmeld — Phase 6c: Themed Packs

## Why this phase
The free daily is the ritual; themed packs are the content depth heavy players binge and the first real money (the stubbed themed-packs IAP made real). They also sharpen both wedges: India-culture packs no global competitor can match, and universal brain-game packs for the global (NYT-Games/Wordle) audience.

## The key design constraint (shapes the whole feature)
The semantic engine works on **guessable common nouns with rich embedding neighborhoods** — NOT proper nouns. "Sholay", "Virat Kohli", individual movie/actor names have sparse, weird neighborhoods and play badly. Therefore a themed pack is **themed common-noun secrets + themed clues**, not proper-noun trivia. The theme lives in the CLUE and the curation; the secret stays a guessable word.
- Bollywood → secret `romance`, clue "The masala every blockbuster runs on."
- Cricket → secret `boundary`, clue "Four runs the easy way, along the ground."
- Space → secret `gravity`, clue "The invisible pull that keeps your feet down."
Pack secrets must be common nouns with good neighborhoods AND present in vocab (extend + eval-gate per pack, exactly like the Hinglish vocab additions).

## Pack model
- A pack = an **on-demand library of ~25 themed puzzles** (secret + clue + rival + par + maxGuesses), reusing the entire existing gameplay + payload machinery.
- Played on-demand (NOT daily-gated) — packs are the binge/retry outlet, like practice/archive. The free daily stays one-per-day (scarcity/ritual preserved).
- Per-pack progress/completion tracked SEPARATELY from the daily streak (packs never affect the daily streak/stats).
- Each pack has its own **distinct visual identity** — icon + accent color, consistent within the pack (per project UI rule; never reuse another pack's/logo). Applied to the pack tile, pack header, and its puzzle screens' accent.

## Commercial model
- **Daily puzzle:** unchanged, free.
- **Packs:** themed-packs IAP on native (per-pack, e.g. ₹49–99 / $0.99–1.99, or an "all packs" bundle — price TBD from funnel data). FREE on web during validation (monetization flags off on web, consistent with all prior phases).
- **Flagship pack (Bollywood): FREE** on all platforms — proves value, most shareable, demonstrates the India wedge.
- Packs are content assets; the system must make adding a new pack a pure content drop (no engineering).

## Full lineup (roadmap — BUILD scope is the system + Bollywood only this phase)

### India wedge (cultural resonance)
1. **Bollywood** — romance, drama, dance, song, villain, hero, tears, interval, dialogue, wedding… (FREE flagship, EN + Hinglish)
2. **Cricket** — bat, wicket, boundary, catch, pitch, captain, victory, stadium, umpire…
3. **Indian Food** — spice, curry, sweet, pickle, feast, masala, tandoor, thali… (needs vocab additions)
4. **Big Fat Indian Wedding** — mehendi, baraat, feast, gold, ceremony, family, dance… (highly shareable)

### Global wedge (universal brain-game appeal — NYT-Games/Wordle demographic)
5. **Nature & Wild** — ocean, forest, storm, predator, migration, canyon, glacier, coral… (broadest funnel, family-friendly, richest vocab)
6. **Space & Science** — star, gravity, orbit, comet, galaxy, energy, atom, eclipse… (captures the trivia/brain-game crowd)
7. **The Movies** (global cinema, as film concepts) — plot, twist, villain, hero, sequel, climax, screen… (follow-on)
8. **Music** — rhythm, melody, chorus, beat, harmony, lyric… (follow-on)

## THIS BUILD (6c) scope
1. **Pack system** (the engineering):
   - Pack manifest + payload namespacing (each pack published like a mini-payload set under its own key, reusing the generator/hosting pipeline).
   - Pack selector screen (grid of pack tiles, each with its own icon/accent, free vs locked state, progress indicator).
   - On-demand pack play: pick a pack → pick/continue a puzzle in it → full existing gameplay (clue, budget, rival, reveal, heat-trail share). Share card notes the pack name.
   - Per-pack progress + completion, persisted separately; never touches the daily streak.
   - IAP wiring: packs behind the existing themed-packs IAP on native (real entitlement per pack), FREE on web; Bollywood free everywhere. Flags default-off on web as always.
   - Per-pack visual identity (icon + accent), Bollywood's designed distinctly (film/spotlight motif, gold/crimson — distinct from the app's core identity).
2. **Bollywood flagship pack, fully authored** (~25 secrets, EN + Hinglish):
   - Themed common-noun secrets (guessable, good neighborhoods, in vocab or vocab-extended + eval-gated).
   - Bollywood-flavored clues per secret, per language (Hinglish clues written for an Indian player, not translated) — CC drafts, GG reviews (STOP POINT), same as the core clue authoring.
   - Rival paths + par + maxGuesses generated by the existing pipeline.
   - Spoiler gate on clues (no clue contains its secret/alias/variant).
3. **Scaffold the next packs** as content-drop stubs: manifest entries + empty schedule/clue files for Cricket, Indian Food, Nature & Wild, so adding each is "author + review + publish," no code.

## Out of scope (content drops after 6c)
Cricket, Indian Food, Wedding, Nature & Wild, Space & Science, Movies, Music full authoring — each its own author→review→publish cycle once the system ships.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Pack system: selector renders pack tiles with per-pack icon/accent, free/locked states, progress; navigating into a pack lists its puzzles.
2. On-demand pack play uses the full existing loop (clue, budget, rival, reveal, heat-trail share); share card shows the pack name; spoiler-safe.
3. Per-pack progress/completion persists separately; daily streak/stats are provably unaffected by pack play (test asserts this).
4. IAP: packs gated by the themed-packs entitlement on native (Bollywood free), FREE on web (flags off); all existing IAP tests stay green.
5. Bollywood pack: ~25 authored secrets + clues (EN + Hinglish), clue spoiler gate passes, rival/par/maxGuesses generated, payload sizes within gates, byte-reproducible.
6. Bollywood has a distinct visual identity (icon + accent) applied consistently; not a reuse of the core app logo.
7. Adding a future pack is a content-only operation (demonstrated by the scaffolded stubs loading gracefully when empty).
8. Full suite green (generator + Dart); new tests for pack selection, pack progress isolation from daily, pack payload schema, IAP gating.
9. Live verify on playmindmeld.vercel.app: open the pack selector, play a full Bollywood puzzle (EN and Hinglish), win + share showing the pack name, and confirm the daily streak is untouched.

## Risks
- **Clue authoring is again the make-or-break content lever** — GG review mandatory (stop point). Themed clues must be flavorful but still solvable and spoiler-free.
- **Themed vocab additions** (tandoor, wicket, etc.) — some theme words aren't in base vocab; extend + eval-gate per pack, watch neighborhood quality.
- **Proper-noun temptation** — do NOT let pack secrets drift into proper nouns; keep them guessable common nouns (design constraint above).
- **Pack scope creep** — build the system + one pack; resist authoring all packs at once.
